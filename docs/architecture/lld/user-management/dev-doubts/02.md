- Something brought up by my tech-lead, but the concept they proposed was that master would own what possible list of sub-modules/features/permissions there are for a module (like create, update, read, export, import, etc) that is set in configurator for a tenant. Basically, this is for what the tenant has access to (above the user). One of the reasons he mentioned master owning the data for what sub-modules/features/permissions exist (not where/how they're assigned) is that the other modules may not exist yet at that point and didn't want to wait on development of the modules yet. So, the flow was like this, when a tenant is being onboarded in configurator then they pull this list of sub-modules/features/permissions from master and then assign them to the tenant. For example, if OPD has registration and also a smart-parcha feature (hypothetically) with a visit sub-feature, then this would be stored in master, pulled during configurator onboarding, and then assigned to the tenant.
